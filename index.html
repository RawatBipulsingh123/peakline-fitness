        Before you come in

        Quick answers. Anything else, message us on WhatsApp.

        What happens in the free trial?

        A coach shows you around and you join one session. Pick a class from the timetable or just come in and we will place you.

        Can I move to a longer plan later?

        Yes. Start monthly, then switch to a 3-month or 12-month plan whenever you are ready.

        How do shop orders and payment work?

        Your order arrives on WhatsApp. We confirm stock and the total there, then arrange payment and delivery with you.

        What should I bring?

        Water, comfortable shoes and a towel. If you forget, the shop sells towels and shakers.
